About SignalNook

Who publishes SignalNook, what the site covers and its independence.

SignalNook is a small, independently maintained educational publication devoted to practical home Wi-Fi decisions. Our purpose is to help readers identify where their connection is failing, collect useful evidence and avoid unnecessary equipment purchases. We are not an internet service provider, router manufacturer or device repair business.

How the site is organized

Every guide begins with a recognizable problem, then explains a test that separates likely causes. Our interactive tools are deliberately limited: they do not request credentials, run device scans or claim they can measure a household network from a browser. The site is designed for people who need a careful next step more than a list of products.

Limits of our work

We do not have access to your router, ISP account, connection logs or home floor plan. Manufacturer interfaces vary, and changing network security or provider settings can interrupt essential devices. Use the instructions for your exact model and request help from your ISP or a qualified network technician when a fix involves unfamiliar router administration, wiring or managed equipment.

Contact and transparency

Suggestions, corrections and accessibility feedback can be sent to raflipanel2@gmail.com. Our editorial process, primary sources and advertising disclosure are publicly available. We do not claim professional certifications, lab testing or first-hand product trials that we have not conducted.
