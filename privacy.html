Privacy notice

SignalNook privacy practices for local tools, advertising and third-party resources.

Effective and reviewed: 11 November 2026. SignalNook is an independent informational website. You can read guides without creating an account. We do not operate a server-side account system, contact form, or personal network scanner.

Tools and browser storage

The Connection Pathfinder and Placement Compare calculate recommendations in your browser without submitting answers to our website. Speed Test Log stores the entries you type in your device's local storage. They are not uploaded to SignalNook. You can remove these entries from the tool or by clearing browser storage. Exported CSV files stay under your control.

Advertising and third parties

Our editorial homepage and article pages load a Google AdSense script for publisher verification and potential advertising. If advertising is enabled, Google may process information and use cookies or similar technologies according to its policies, subject to applicable consent requirements and available settings. We do not claim to control Google's data collection. Learn more in our advertising disclosure and Google's partner-site information.

External links and fonts

This site links to external official support documentation. Visiting those sites is governed by their privacy practices. The site requests fonts from Google Fonts; this can result in network requests to Google's services. You may use browser privacy tools and controls according to your preferences.

Logs and hosting

The site's hosting provider and delivery infrastructure may process standard technical request information for site delivery and security. We do not publish a detailed retention claim for those systems because it depends on the hosting configuration in use. We do not intentionally collect Wi-Fi passwords, router configuration backups or precise home addresses.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as site features change. Privacy questions can be sent to raflipanel2@gmail.com.
