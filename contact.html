Contact SignalNook

Contact SignalNook with corrections, suggestions and accessibility feedback.

We welcome clear, specific feedback about our educational articles, broken links, accessibility issues or possible factual errors.

Email

Write to raflipanel2@gmail.com. If reporting a content issue, include the guide URL, the statement you believe is inaccurate and, where possible, an official reference. We do not guarantee a response time or provide individual technical support.

Protect your privacy

Please do not send Wi-Fi passwords, router administrative credentials, full network diagnostic reports, serial numbers or personal addresses. If a diagnostic excerpt is essential, remove identifying network names, IP addresses and device identifiers before sharing.

For connection outages

If your home has no service, contact your internet service provider or the manufacturer of your router. For suspected compromise, use your vendor's official security support. SignalNook cannot view or repair your connection remotely.
