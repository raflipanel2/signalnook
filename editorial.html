Editorial standards

How SignalNook selects, researches, tests and maintains its home networking guidance.

Our editorial principle is simple: a suggested troubleshooting action should answer a specific question. We favor comparing two devices, testing the same location, or checking wired versus wireless performance over changing several settings and guessing which change helped.

Sources and attribution

We prioritize primary guidance from hardware and operating-system vendors, consumer protection organizations and established technical references. Links are provided where relevant in individual guides. Where a feature or menu depends on a model, firmware version or regulatory region, we avoid universal instructions and encourage readers to consult official device-specific documentation.

What we do not claim

We do not independently measure router performance or certify network security. Planner scores are explicitly heuristic, and a saved speed-test value is a user-entered observation rather than a result measured by SignalNook. The absence of affiliate product links reflects our focus on understanding network behavior rather than ranking purchases.

Corrections, updates and dates

Editorial dates indicate a material review of content, not automatic freshness. If a guide becomes inaccurate after a router, browser or operating-system update, we aim to amend the relevant steps and explain material changes. Please report specific errors with the guide URL to raflipanel2@gmail.com. We review feedback but cannot provide individualized network administration or emergency support.

Safety and user control

Our guides encourage reversible tests, documentation before resets, strong Wi-Fi encryption and authorized administration only. We do not publish instructions for accessing networks without permission or bypassing local radio-spectrum restrictions.
